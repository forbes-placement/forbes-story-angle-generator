## Overview

Forbes Story Angle Generator is an AI-powered platform that helps businesses, founders, executives, and marketing teams develop compelling editorial story ideas for premium business publications.

By analysing a brand's industry, achievements, target audience, and business objectives, the platform generates strategic story angles designed to capture media attention and strengthen brand authority.

Built for PR professionals, startups, agencies, and growing businesses, the platform simplifies editorial planning with intelligent recommendations tailored to each brand. Whether preparing for a Forbes feature, executive profile, innovation story, or industry insight article, Forbes Story Angle Generator helps transform business achievements into engaging narratives that support thought leadership, media visibility, and long-term brand credibility.

---

## Features

- Story Angle Quality Score — evaluates strength, originality, and editorial appeal
- Editorial Fit Score — measures alignment with Forbes and premium publication standards
- Brand Authority Score — assesses thought leadership positioning and credibility
- Media Attention Score — predicts likelihood of capturing editorial and journalist interest
- Narrative Strength Score — evaluates storytelling depth and engagement potential
- SEO & Visibility Score — measures search and digital discoverability

```bash

```

### Node.js CLI
